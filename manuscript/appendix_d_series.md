# Appendix D: The Geometric Series — Domain Instantiations

This book is the parent text of a series. Each domain book instantiates the general framework on a domain-specific manifold, inheriting the heuristic field formalism, the geodesic deviation measure, the failure taxonomy, the gauge invariance diagnostics, and the engineering toolkit without re-derivation.

## Published

**Geometric Methods in Computational Modeling** (Bond, 2026a). The mathematical toolkit: manifolds, metrics, curvature, persistent homology, group-theoretic augmentation, SPD manifolds, TDA, hyperbolic geometry, the Bond Geodesic Equilibrium. Provides the apparatus; does not commit it to any subject.

**Geometric Ethics: The Mathematical Structure of Moral Reasoning** (Bond, 2026b). The first domain instantiation. Moral reasoning on a 9-dimensional Whitney-stratified manifold. Obligation vectors, interest covectors, the $D_4 \times U(1)_H$ gauge group, conservation of harm, the No Escape Theorem. 30 chapters, 400+ pages. Demonstrates the full depth that domain instantiation can achieve. [github.com/ahb-sjsu/geometric-ethics]

## The Unifying Question

Each book asks the same question: *what structure does scalar reduction destroy in this domain, and what does geometry recover?*

The parent theory provides the answer template. The domain books fill in the details. The accumulation of domain instantiations — each tested against its own data, each confirming or falsifying the prediction that the architecture transfers — is the empirical program of the series.
